    Before you book

    How do I book?

    Message us on WhatsApp with the service and the day that suits you, and we'll suggest a time.

    How much do highlights cost?

    Highlights start from AED 800. The final price depends on your hair length and thickness. Send a daylight photo of your hair on WhatsApp and Omran will give you a price range.

    How do the packages work?

    Book three sessions of the same Moroccan bath or the same massage, and the fourth is free. Bath packages are valid for 3 months. The Royal Bride bath is not included.

    Is the villa for ladies only?

    Yes. 33 Beauty Centre is a ladies' salon.
